## 1. Relation and total-order IOP

The statement is the fixed profile label, its canonical public preprocessing commitment, and96 canonical u16 fields encoding a64-byte root,64-byte nullifier and64-byte context digest. Its byte encoding is injective within this fixed relation; there is no caller-selected circuit or verifier key. The witness contains a64-byte secret,32 ordered64-byte siblings and32 Boolean directions, together with the satisfying Keccak/auxiliary trace. The AIR requires SHA3-512(secret) to be the leaf,32 correctly ordered SHA3-512 Merkle steps to produce the root, and SHA3-512(secret[0..32]) to equal the nullifier. Context columns equal all32 context fields on the first row and remain equal across transitions. Pool policy derives the context; the AIR binds its bytes without authorizing arbitrary caller-selected policy.

The IOP uses Goldilocks p=18446744069414584321, E=Fp[X]/(X^5−3), an original2048-row trace, randomized polynomial dimension K=4096, LDE size n=65536,16 quotient chunks, binary FRI,320 queries and a constant final polynomial. Every PoW budget is zero. There is no grinding security credit.

Use a total order with32 messages: prover message2r and verifier message2r+1, for r=0,...,15. A total order satisfies GordieHowe Definition2.18, with no merges and nJ=0. The16 verifier messages are alpha, zeta, gamma, twelve FRI betas, and **one joint vector** of320 indices. Consecutive values of an extension challenge form one E-valued verifier message, not five independently salted messages.

The initial prover message carries its trace oracle. The next carries the quotient and independently committed randomizer oracles; multiple oracle strings in one prover message are explicitly permitted. The sixteen same-height quotient matrices are one product-alphabet oracle: one position holds their complete ordered tuple. Its leaf encoding includes the fixed-width rows and their salt tuples in the native order. Fixed widths make this an injective codec. Subsequent non-oracle opening claims and FRI oracle commitments have the fixed native schedule. Random columns and masks are included in the committed alphabets; extraction discards auxiliary columns after recovering the source polynomial trace.

Queries are determined solely by the verifier challenges. Repeated query indices are normalized to unique positions per oracle; arithmetic may revisit the same authenticated value. Section5 gives the binding reduction for the EVM's expanded openings. Public preprocessing is handled separately in Section4.

## 2. Native round-by-round knowledge and whole-view HVZK

These are arguments about the oracle IOP, before Merkle or Fiat–Shamir compilation. Passing native or EVM tests is not their justification.

**Polynomial candidate and zero-correlation extraction.** In the zero-correlated opening protocol, for each source column w and claimed opening v at z_j, the corresponding DEEP word `(w(X)−v)/(X−z_j)` must be a polynomial of degree<K−1. Equality on the entire LDE domain then forces `w(X)=(X−z_j)u(X)+v` there. More than K points determine a unique degree<K source polynomial. Requests at both zeta and g·zeta therefore identify the same source candidate; the extractor does not choose a new candidate at each check. Base-valued source words give base-field coefficients, by comparing the candidate with its Frobenius conjugate on more points than its degree.

Evaluate the recovered trace polynomial on the original2048-point subgroup and deinterleave its original rows. When all3744 AIR constraints vanish, those rows yield the relation's secret, siblings and Boolean directions. This is a concrete polynomial-time extractor; its running time need not be practical. The fixed public preprocessing is the actual public table, as ensured by Section4.

If the candidate violates a constraint, some original row has a nonzero constraint vector. Consecutive-powers batching gives a nonzero polynomial in alpha of degree<3744 at that row. Outside fewer than3744 exceptional alphas, the collapsed numerator is not divisible by the original trace zerofier. No quotient chosen after alpha can repair that divisibility failure as a polynomial identity. Once the source and quotient words are fixed, a false recomposition identity is a nonzero polynomial in zeta, conservatively of degree≤69633. True opening claims at a nonroot of that polynomial make the scalar check reject. False claims instead make at least one source DEEP word nonpolynomial. This defines the zero-correlated doomed-state test and a candidate-preserving extractor, using only the existing prefix. A prefix already yielding a valid decoded trace is extractable immediately.

**Correlated agreement and FRI.** Inflate the degree ratio conservatively to `rho=(K+2)/n`, take agreement `a=(1+rho)/2=34817/65536`, and `eta=a−sqrt(rho)`. This is strictly inside the unique-decoding regime for the actual degree<K words. Their jointly agreeing tuple is unique. The powers-curve correlated-agreement theorem bounds exceptional source-batching gammas by `(5652−1)n/|E|`; it covers consecutive powers, rather than assuming an independently uniform coefficient vector. The binary folding exceptions sum conservatively to131052/|E|. Conditional on remaining outside those exceptional events,320 independent uniform16-bit indices all land on an accepting agreement set with probability at most a^320. Sampling with replacement is included in this calculation.

Block et al. Theorem4.6 preserves **RBR knowledge**, not merely language soundness, when this correlated-agreement protocol replaces the zero-correlated check. Its lifting loss is `1/(2 eta sqrt(rho))<8` at these parameters. Lemma4.8 composes the causal doomed states of the opening/identity and correlated-agreement stages without replacing their source candidate. A conservative common per-round bound is therefore

`epsilon_k <= 8 * [3744/|E| + 69633/|E| + 5651*n/|E| + 131052/|E| + a^320]`.

This is a finite IOP error ledger, not an implemented-protocol security-bit label. Padding the sums and using8 instead of the smaller exact lifting factor only weakens the bound. Under a total order, GordieHowe Lemma2.21 identifies the ordinary prefix/doom-state formulation with its causal Definition2.20. The extractor and state here read the relevant prefix, so the later compiler's RBR premise is the knowledge premise required there.

**Whole-view hiding, including FRI.** Original trace columns have2048 independent base-field mask coefficients, equivalently w+Z_H·r. The quotient split has fifteen independent chunk masks and a balanced last chunk. Charge Frobenius-coordinate disclosures, not just one extension evaluation: the source trace budget is at most690 and each quotient mask budget at most325, both below2048. Haböck–Al Kindi's trace/splitting argument therefore hides the source openings with the conservatively enlarged point sets. The public preprocessing contains no private values.

It remains necessary to hide the entire combined DEEP polynomial, because merely hiding its sampled cells would not justify all FRI commitments and the final coefficient. Here is the relevant rank calculation. Condition on zeta and gamma having full degree5 and on the disclosed OOD source/randomizer values. Let P_z be zeta's degree5 minimal polynomial. For each of the first five independent base-field randomizer columns, its remaining difference is `P_z·S_j`, with S_j uniform of degree<K−5. The five powers1,gamma,...,gamma^4 are an Fp basis of E. Their combination therefore fills E[X]_<K−5 uniformly, coefficient by coefficient. Dividing by X−zeta gives exactly the space of degree<K−1 E-polynomials vanishing at zeta's other four conjugates.

Every difference between source DEEP contributions of witnesses having the same disclosures lies in that same space. Base-column differences vanish on the full Frobenius orbit of zeta. Terms divided by X−g·zeta are regular on this orbit: g·zeta cannot be a conjugate of zeta, since taking norms would imply g^5=1 although g has order2048. Thus the combined DEEP polynomial is uniform on a witness-independent affine coset, conditional on the already disclosed values. Its four prescribed conjugate values are computable from those values alone.

The HVZK simulator first samples those source disclosures using the trace/quotient mask maps, then samples that affine coset, and computes the honest folds and final coefficient. The resulting adaptive query set is independent of the private source witness under this conditioning. Conditioning on the chosen combined polynomial does not bias the source distribution: each source choice has an equal-size randomizer fiber. Source openings at the eventual query set can consequently be simulated by the same mask maps. At each base query point, the combined-polynomial equation and the gamma basis determine the first five randomizer row values from public/simulated quantities and the remaining independent randomizer values; the other randomizer coefficients are uniform in the residual linear fibers.

For quotient-coordinate disclosures, simulate the first fifteen chunk masks at all five conjugates. The five reconstructed quotient identities determine the last chunk there; invert the Moore-basis map to recover its five base-coordinate OOD claims. This uses the same disclosed trace/shifted values as the identities, rather than a fresh independent witness candidate. All these systems have more mask coefficients than constraints. The exceptional event that zeta or gamma lies in Fp costs at most2/p^4; ordinary completeness exclusions can also be charged. This supplies statistical whole-view oracle HVZK under ideal independent masks. Fresh private seeded generators require the computational hybrid described below.

## 3. Exact two-layer compiler and sampler

Native source: `strong_config.rs` and `strong_transcript.rs`. There is one fresh OS-generated uniform64-byte proof salt s. There are no independent per-coordinate or per-round salts. Let r be the logical verifier round. The challenge-key input is exactly:

`QRAFT-SPBCS-CH-V5\0 || s64 || statementLength:u32BE || statement || (2r+1):u32BE || itemCount:u32BE || (itemIndex:u32BE || itemLength:u64BE || itemBytes)*`.

The statement is `QRAFT-WITHDRAWAL-V5/q320-b16-e5\0 || canonicalPreprocessingRoot64 || public96LEu64`. Public fields must be96 canonical u16 values. Prover item2r is the unmodified P3-observed byte record; record0 does not prepend another profile. Earlier verifier item2j+1 is the first64 natural-order bytes of its stream. Every prior item is retained with its index, so the encoding is injective. The fixed schedule determines all types and lengths.

The64-byte key is BLAKE2b-512 of that input. The stream is cSHAKE256 with empty function-name N, customization `QRAFT-SPBCS-STREAM-V5`, input `key64 || s64`, and output length L=2560 bytes. Its first64 bytes are written back **before the next prover record**. This is the key/stream/write-back structure of Protocol6.5. AppendixI.1 itself implements short digests and streams using different concrete hash functions.

We instantiate b=64 bytes and e=2496 bytes. In the ideal model, use one L-byte-output oracle on four injectively encoded disjoint input domains. The short BLAKE interfaces expose its first64 bytes on ch/leaf/node inputs; the cSHAKE interface exposes L bytes on stream inputs. An ideal short quantum query is simulated with at most two full-oracle queries, computing into scratch, copying the digest and uncomputing scratch. Thus count at most a factor2 in oracle-query budgets. Replacing these ideal restrictions with BLAKE2b/cSHAKE is an explicit **joint random-oracle instantiation assumption**, not a consequence of collision resistance alone.

Native SerializingChallenger64 pops bytes from the end of the complete stream. Formally the sampler reads the whole fixed-length stream, then applies that suffix-order function. Reading the whole stream is reading a prefix of length L, so the AppendixI.1 prefix formulation does not exclude this sampler. More importantly, the actual hypotheses—equal preimage cardinalities and polynomial-time conditional-preimage sampling—hold directly, as follows.

For an extension challenge, divide the stream into320 reverse-consumed64-bit words. Each is uniform in[0,2^64). Reject words≥p until five canonical words have been accepted; fail if all320 words are exhausted first. For any target vector in Fp^5, the number of streams stopping at word n is

`C(n−1,4) * (2^64−p)^(n−5) * (2^64)^(320−n)`, for5≤n≤320.

This count is independent of the target vector. To sample a uniformly random preimage, choose n with these integer weights, choose its four earlier acceptance positions uniformly, place the five target coordinates in order, fill rejected positions uniformly in[p,2^64), and fill unread words uniformly. The counting integers have O(L) bits, with at most320 cases, so counting/indexing is polynomial. This is precisely a finite conditional-preimage sampler, not a heuristic invocation of rejection sampling. The overrun probability is bounded by `C(320,4)*(1−p/2^64)^316`: if at most four successes occur, at least316 rejected positions form one of that many subsets. Sum this bound over the fifteen extension messages.

The last message consumes exactly320 words and takes each low16 bits. Every target index vector has exactly `2^(48*320)` preimages. Conditional preimages are sampled by filling those low bits and drawing all higher bits independently. Its overrun is zero. It is one verifier challenge; splitting it into independently grindable subchallenges would be a different construction. Stream exhaustion, incomplete or excess logical sampling, and incomplete16-move transcripts reject.

## 4. Public preprocessing and finite-field leaf salts

The private leaf salt space is uniform Fp^16 (or its larger product for a combined leaf), not uniform128-byte strings. The [finite-support lemma](pq-finite-field-salt-lemma.md) proves directly, for M=p^16 and R=2^512,

`E_H max_m TV(P_(H,m),U_R) <= 1/2*sqrt((R−1)/M) + sqrt(ln N/(2M))`.

The dominant term is approximately2^-257. The proof replaces joint salt/digest pairs, preserving conditional preimages, so later adaptive openings and salt disclosure are covered by data processing even with whole-oracle quantum side information. There is no2^-28 uniform-byte replacement loss. Union over private oracle positions supplies the leaf term in the compiler's ZK hybrid. It changes that elementary bound's cardinality parameter without changing the hash primitive.

**Public-branch lemma.** The preprocessing table and its salts derive from public fixed seeds and the fixed public setup algorithm. They are not fresh secret commitments. Compute its full public LDE and its expected salted Merkle tree in a public setup phase. The table is H-independent arithmetic/public-PRG data; its Merkle root is the result of public H queries. One65536-leaf binary tree uses131071 leaf/internal-node queries; reserve q_setup≤131072. Its computed root is included in the statement and fixed by the verifier/pool deployment profile.

Replace every accepted preprocessing authentication path by a lookup of the corresponding canonical public row. If its row or salt differs, compare its root path against the canonical tree, starting at the equal root and descending to the first difference. Either two distinct internal-node inputs have one digest, or distinct leaf inputs have one digest. This produces a collision in the512-bit commitment digest. Thus, outside that collision event, the lookup-normalized verifier has exactly the same accepted statements and arithmetic values. This is computational binding of public data, not statistical hiding of its deterministic salts.

In the normalized IOP, the verifier computes public rows itself. They are not private prover-oracle positions, so they are absent from npos and from the fresh-salt hybrid. The preprocessing OOD claims still participate in the DEEP consistency check against these known rows, which forces the same public polynomial candidate. Adding a publicly computable lookup to a polynomial-time IOP verifier does not require a new hidden-oracle compiler: its queries remain challenge-determined, and there is no prover message for that table. The root is extra public statement metadata whose value does not alter the canonical AIR/table. The theorem permits adaptive statements; charge the setup queries before selecting that metadata rather than conditioning a fresh oracle on an unexplained hardcoded root. This resolves the deterministic public branch by an explicit lookup/binding reduction, not by labeling its salts uniform.

### Explicit wrapper proposition (public-branch bookkeeping closed)

Let R' have statements `x'=(root,nullifier,context,profile,prepRootMetadata)`. Its membership decider checks the fixed SHA3-note/AIR relation and ignores prepRootMetadata. Define IOP' to perform the same ALI/DEEP/FRI arithmetic, with the canonical public preprocessing row computed by its verifier whenever needed. The OOD preprocessing claims remain untrusted non-oracle messages and are still checked by DEEP/FRI; an early direct OOD equality check is not required. The metadata and its typed P3 seed bytes remain in the statement/messages, but do not choose the public polynomial oracle. Thus R' is independent of the proof random oracle. The RBR extraction and HVZK arguments of Section2 apply to this fixed-public-source case; no private preprocessing mask or salt is being assumed.

Construct wrapper adversary B as follows. It starts with code, seed conventions and auxiliary state independent of H. It computes the canonical public tree with H using at most q_setup queries, gives its resulting root/profile to A, and forwards A's statement/proof queries as x' with that root metadata. On A's target proof it performs the accepted-path normalization just proved and strips redundant public authentication. B therefore chooses its statements **after** these counted oracle queries, which is expressly allowed by the adaptive-statement theorem. There is no conditioning on an unexplained pre-existing H-dependent auxiliary input, and no oracle-dependent relation decider.

Outside the extracted public-path collision event, the normalized native verifier and compiled IOP' verifier make identical arithmetic decisions. For honest proofs this is exact. For simulation, the GordieHowe simulator programs only STREAM points; the typed LEAF/NODE public tree does not change, so attach the same deterministic public authentication material on either side of the hybrid. This attachment and stripping are polynomial-time wrappers, with explicit setup/path-query costs. Applying the core compiler theorem to B and lifting through these wrappers therefore costs only the stated binding advantage and query overhead. This closes the deterministic public-branch bookkeeping; it is not a new assumption about independent preprocessing roots or a new compiler primitive.

## 5. Repeated queries and staged EVM normalization

Definition2.18 queries unique oracle positions. Deduplicate the320-index vector separately for each source oracle and FRI layer, retaining the original vector for arithmetic. Native MMCS pruned proofs already deduplicate. For the staged EVM's separate paths, collect the accepted paths under each fixed root and position. If two paths disagree on a leaf or an overlapping internal node, the same root-to-first-difference comparison yields a commitment collision. Otherwise the paths merge into one consistent tree fragment and a canonical unique-position multiproof. The arithmetic sees the same row every time it revisits that position.

This gives a polynomial-time normalization from any accepted expanded staged proof to a Protocol6.5 proof, outside an explicit commitment-collision event. It also handles permissionless callers submitting different stages: they cannot select challenge indices, source roots, AIR programs, alpha, zeta or gamma as authority. Source/FRI roots are prover messages fixed before the relevant key; all key inputs are reconstructed from the fixed statement and immutable records. Each batch authenticates its exact rows/values against those roots and indices. Final acceptance requires16 moves,93 pinned AIR batches,21 projected claims,320 input reductions,6080 authenticated matrices and12 complete FRI rounds. The consumer and all192 statement bytes are checked again at consumption; pool replay/expiry/value policy is independent.

Ordinary EVM runtime code hashes identify deployed implementations; they are not substitutes for the512-bit proof commitments. Implementation correctness, actual deployment pins, state transitions and absence of alternate acceptance paths remain required verification obligations. Foundry/native parity establishes finite execution evidence, not those mathematical theorems by itself.

## 6. Applying the compiler and custody reduction

For the normalized private-oracle schedule, npos=3*65536+sum_(h=4)^15 2^h=262128. Nmsg=32, maximum two oracles per message, c=64 and nJ=0. Unique query positions are at most3*320 +7*320+256+128+64+32+16=3696. GordieHowe's conservative formulas give kq≤66592 and qS≤524288. Add public-branch authentication work and any helper-hash work to the verifier/simulator query budgets; include q_setup in the preprocessing budget. For example, public preprocessing paths add at most320*17=5440 queries per run. Counting more only weakens the bound.

With those augmented budgets and the at-most-two-query short-oracle simulation, apply Theorem6.6(3)/I.5 to the whole-view HVZK argument, replacing the byte-salt leaf term by the proved finite-support term. The proof of its leaf replacement uses exactly the joint-pair cardinality lemma, so no new compiler primitive is being assumed. This yields the stated adaptive proof-oracle ZK bound, including proof-salt collisions/hiding, sampler overrun, HVZK and private-leaf terms. Replace ideal mask/salt streams by the concrete independently seeded private generators through explicit quantum-PRG distinguishing hybrids. This gives conditional computational QPT ZK; it does not give everlasting statistical privacy for a finite-seed implementation against unlimited future computation.

Apply Theorem6.6(5)/I.17 with the RBR knowledge extractor from Section2. It supplies weak online simulation extraction for a statement not previously queried to the proof simulator, including the adversary's final auxiliary state. In the paper's notation, `k=kq+Q*qS`, `T=t+k+Q*qS`, and the extraction bound is

`2*sqrt(k/2^(8L)) + 6*T^2*(epsilon_k + (max(2,c)+nJ+1)*T/2^(8b))`.

Use augmented t,kq,qS for setup/helper work and the ideal-oracle simulation overhead, not the unaugmented numbers above. Add the normalization/public-branch collision errors; they are quantum collision-resistance advantages, conventionally O(q_total^3/2^512) in the ideal512-bit digest model, with all reduction queries counted. Reusing a shared global leaf/node domain is exactly the GordieHowe layout, rather than the different indexed-VC compiler route.

For custody, define an unauthorized fresh-context withdrawal as one spending an honest unspent deposited note to a context its owner did not authorize. Replace earlier honest proofs by the adaptive ZK simulator, charging its distinguishing error. A different recipient/relayer/fee/deadline yields a different full statement unless the pool's BLAKE2b-512 context encoding collides. Weak simulation extraction therefore supplies a satisfying note/path witness for an accepted fresh-context forgery. Its state-preserving property allows the reduction to continue with that witness.

Compare the extracted authentication path with the public deposited tree for its known historical root. A disagreement yields a SHA3-512 tree/leaf collision; otherwise it identifies a deposited commitment. A different secret for that same commitment yields a SHA3 collision. If it identifies the honest target note, extracting its secret inverts the **joint** function

`F(s64)=(SHA3-512(s64), SHA3-512(s64[0..32]))`.

Assume this concrete joint function remains one-way against quantum polynomial-time algorithms given its two outputs and independently generated public data. After the ZK hybrid, all prior-proof auxiliary information is efficiently publicly simulable from those outputs and statements; the assumption does not grant real witness-dependent proofs as unexplained auxiliary advice. The nullifier is already part of the challenge leakage; ordinary one-wayness of the leaf hash without it is insufficient. With several honest notes, selecting the challenged note incurs the usual explicit multi-target/guessing factor. An extracted attacker-owned unspent note is a legitimate withdrawal rather than theft. A previously spent note is rejected by the global nullifier, and a second secret with a different nullifier but the same leaf again requires a collision.

The SHA3 input lengths32,64 and128 distinguish nullifier, secret commitment and tree-node roles. A random-oracle heuristic for these restricted input-length domains suggests the expected postquantum preimage behavior, but the actual AIR computes concrete SHA3; replacing it by a random function would change that relation. The defensible assumption is the concrete joint one-way/collision property, not a claim that the AIR proves a different oracle computation. Its first32 secret bytes contain256 bits of entropy, so a512-bit digest does not imply256-bit quantum preimage work for this leakage pattern.

Finally, the same full statement can be copied but cannot redirect payment: the pool derives its context from chain, pool, asset, denomination, recipient, relayer, fee and deadline; changing any bound field requires a context collision. Consumption is pool-only and one-time, and the global nullifier prevents duplicate payout across sessions. These are application-state checks, not consequences of weak simulation extraction alone.

## 7. Exact claim and implementation status

The argument above gives a conditional route from the fixed IOP's stated algebraic premises through the specified two-layer compiler and normalization to quantum knowledge, QPT ZK and fresh-context authorization. It names concrete hash/PRG and joint-note-one-way assumptions and counts public setup; it does not require a new hash construction or an independent machine proof as an extra cryptographic assumption.

Native evidence includes a genuine v5 proof, all16 exact prover/verifier key/stream logs, independent BLAKE/cSHAKE recomputation, public/salt/envelope mutations and transcript progress tests. Independent source and mathematical reviews found no concrete blocking error. The actual deposited note's v5 proof then completed on chain8141:724 verification transactions and one exact0.0001ETH withdrawal all confirmed. Exact receipts and measurements are in [pq-hiding-stark-v5-live-evidence.json](pq-hiding-stark-v5-live-evidence.json).

Neither v3 nor v4 automatically inherits this compiler/SSE result. Archived native-generation summaries retain their generation-time compiler/custody flags; current reviewed scope and live status are recorded separately in the V5 evidence document. They are not authority for on-chain acceptance.

No claim here covers the devnet's consensus, EOA transaction signatures used for test deployment/carriage, endpoint anonymity, browser compromise, leaked note secrets, paymaster solvency or denial of service. Those layers do not become postquantum merely because proof verification uses this scheme. No numerical128-bit certification follows without the full chosen adversary budgets, theorem constants, all hybrids and concrete implementation review.
