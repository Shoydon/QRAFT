PROBLEMS

Tornado Cash Classic: limitations

Withdrawals depend on a centralised relayer

In the relayer route, a third-party service submits your transaction.

Paying another wallet requires a withdrawal

Funds leave the private pool and arrive at a public address.

Direct RPC requests expose your IP

Onchain privacy does not hide your network identity.

Funds are split into fixed-value notes

Each deposit has its own note; exact payments are awkward.

No built-in post-quantum wallet

Classic does not provide PQ account signatures or receiving encryption.

SOLUTION

What QRAFT adds

One unified private balance

Automatic note selection, exact amounts and private change.

No application relayer

Pay privately without withdrawing

IP protection through Nym

ERC-8286 PQ wallets

ARCHITECTURE

How a private transfer works

01

Sender’s device

Selects notes, encrypts payment and change, generates a local proof.

→
02

Anon RPC + node

Browser submits EIP-8141 frames through Nym. Onchain contracts authorize and sponsor execution.

→
03

Private pool

Verifies proof and nullifiers, records commitments and encrypted notes.

→
04

Recipient’s device

Decrypts its notes, excludes spent notes and calculates the balance.

Sponsored setup and deposit

Factory deploys the ERC-8286 PQ account; paymaster funds stake and gas. Deposit ETH into the pool.

Withdraw from the pool

Proof → pool sends ETH to public address.

Deposit and withdrawal amounts and addresses are public. Private-transfer amounts are hidden by the proof and encrypted notes.
